S-1

SIPOC high-level architecture

One enterprise-level SIPOC, then a SIPOC per Level 1 process with its accountable owner and the indicative Odoo 19 home and fit.

Enterprise SIPOC

SSuppliers

  • Landowners, land brokers, JV partners
  • Banks, Pag-IBIG (HDMF), shareholders
  • Contractors, suppliers, consultants
  • Accredited realty firms and agents
  • BIR, Registry of Deeds, LGUs, DHSUD, DENR

IInputs

  • Land and titles
  • Capital, loans, project funding
  • Materials, labor, designs, permits
  • Market demand, buyers' payments
  • Policies, tax and housing regulations

PProcess

  • Plan & fund (budget, financing)
  • Acquire land
  • Develop (project setup, construction)
  • Stock & sell (inventory, channels, sales, leasing)
  • Collect & finance (monitoring, takeout, IHF, cancellations)
  • Deliver & manage (turnover, title, property management)
  • Pay & measure (AP, forecasts, KPIs)

OOutputs

  • Completed houses, lots and condo units
  • Titles (TCT/CCT) in buyers' names
  • Managed communities, HOA turnover
  • Cash collections, revenue, returns
  • Statutory reports and BIR filings

CCustomers

  • Homebuyers and co-borrowers
  • Tenants
  • Homeowners' associations
  • Shareholders and ManCom
  • Lenders, regulators, auditors

S-2

Cross-departmental process maps

Seven end-to-end BPMN 2.0 collaboration diagrams. PHI is one pool with a lane per department; buyers, lenders, suppliers and government agencies are collapsed (black-box) pools that interact only through message flows.

Notation used

Leasing (10) and corporate financing (02) follow the procure-to-pay and contract-to-cash patterns above and are covered in the SIPOC and matrix; draw them at Level 2 if the BRD needs lane detail.

Lot as the master record

The vendor proposes the Lot as the master model. In this blueprint that means one Lot record per sellable unit, and every process after 07 reads from or writes to it.
